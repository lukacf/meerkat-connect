Readiness is a current role-specific observation. It is not permanent permission or a replacement for per-operation policy.

**Designed contract.** The complete native binding, restoration and frontend lifecycle described here remains pending. Existing component APIs and their narrower evidence are listed in [availability](/availability).

| Observation | Establishes | Does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Package parsed | Metadata fits the selected profile | Executable safety or installation |
| Artifact prepared | Selected bytes are present under the preparation contract | Runtime activation |
| Account authenticated | A selected authentication relationship | Agent permission or full service coverage |
| Process healthy | A component's health condition | Service binding or frontend audience readiness |
| Service ready | Required operations/events are verified and bound for the intended principal/consumer | Completion of the user's business task |
| Frontend ready | Admission, conversation, target and delivery contracts are acknowledged | Unrestricted access to agent history or private services |

## Revision-bound outcomes

Bindings refer to exact package/manifest, connection, grant, target incarnation and effective runtime surface. A late receipt for an old selection cannot activate a replacement. Account change, schema change, revocation or member restoration requires reconciliation with current owners.

Native additions become available at their supported activation boundary. A handshake finishing in the background does not make a tool callable while an agent remains blocked inside its original setup call. The designed workflow yields and resumes the original task through that boundary.

## Combined requests

Report service and frontend outcomes separately. "Documents connected; Slack frontend awaiting administrator installation" is useful progress. A single green badge would hide the unfinished requirement.

Removal hides future use and handles in-flight operations under the declared drain/cancel contract. Removing local access cannot undo external effects that already occurred. Forced or degraded cleanup must remain visible.

## Startup observation

Current released startup behavior and the proposed typed MobKit startup protocol must not be conflated. The proposed acceptance/progress/settled protocol is **unreleased**. Its design declares readiness only from a settled-ready result; progress is not readiness. It has no built-in SDK stall timer. Caller cancellation, a caller deadline or transport loss leaves `InitOutcomeUnknown` when settlement cannot be observed.

Tool presence also needs actual runtime evidence. A host customizer registration or an SDK fake test does not prove the tool survives restoration, delivery repair, helper creation or every spawn path. Observe the actual `identity_resolved_tools` surface and dispatch through the intended member before claiming that binding is usable.
